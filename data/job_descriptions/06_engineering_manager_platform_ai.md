# Engineering Manager - Platform & AI Enablement

**Department:** Engineering Management  
**Seniority:** Lead  
**Location:** Bucharest, Romania / Hybrid  
**Work model:** Hybrid  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English, Romanian

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions is scaling its internal Platform group, which provides cloud infrastructure, developer experience, and AI enablement services to more than 150 engineers. Leadership here is coaching-first: managers stay close to the work while owning outcomes, not tickets.

## About the Role

We are looking for an Engineering Manager for the Platform & AI Enablement team. You will lead 7-10 engineers spanning DevOps, developer tooling, and internal AI services, owning both the people side and the roadmap of the golden path that every product squad builds on.

## Responsibilities

- Lead, coach, and grow a team of 7-10 platform engineers; run 1:1s, career development plans, and performance cycles.
- Own the team roadmap: prioritization, quarterly OKRs, and predictable delivery of platform capabilities.
- Partner with Product and Enterprise Architecture to translate strategy into technical milestones.
- Establish healthy delivery practices: lightweight process, clear definitions of done, and continuous improvement retrospectives.
- Drive the AI enablement program: roll-out and governance of coding assistants, internal LLM gateway, and usage policy.
- Own reliability outcomes (SLOs, incident trends) and foster blameless post-mortem culture.
- Hire aggressively: job design, screening, structured interviews, and onboarding.
- Report progress and risk transparently to the Head of Engineering and stakeholders.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 2+ years of formal people management in a software engineering organization, teams of 5+.
- 5+ years of prior hands-on engineering experience, ideally in platform, DevOps, or backend roles.
- Track record of delivering cross-team roadmaps on time with measurable outcomes.
- Experience introducing AI/developer tooling programs with adoption metrics.
- Excellent written and spoken English; comfortable facilitating meetings and writing decision documents.

## Nice to Have

- Experience in regulated industries (finance, telecom) with audit requirements.
- Familiarity with DORA metrics, SLO frameworks, and incident management processes (ITIL or SRE practices).
- Background in scaling a platform team through rapid headcount growth.
- Technical certifications (CKA, AWS/GCP) are a plus but not required.

## Soft Skills

- Coaching-first leadership; you grow people rather than route around them.
- Strong conflict resolution and stakeholder management.
- Pragmatic optimism: you balance ambition with team sustainability.

## What We Offer

- Hybrid work in Bucharest (2 days on site) with flexible hours.
- Management training budget and executive coaching.
- Competitive salary plus annual bonus tied to team OKRs.
- Private medical insurance, meal cards, and additional parental support.
- Meaningful scope: your decisions shape how 150+ engineers work every day.

Send your CV with a short summary of a team you grew or a roadmap you turned around, including what you learned from what did not go as planned.
