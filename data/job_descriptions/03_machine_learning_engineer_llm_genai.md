# Machine Learning Engineer (LLM & GenAI)

**Department:** AI & Applied Research  
**Seniority:** Mid-Senior  
**Location:** Cluj-Napoca, Romania / Hybrid  
**Work model:** Hybrid  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions runs an Applied AI group that embeds generative AI into enterprise workflows: knowledge assistants, document intelligence, and decision-support tools for regulated industries. We operate our own LLMOps tooling and treat evaluation and governance as first-class citizens.

## About the Role

We are hiring a Machine Learning Engineer to design, build, and operate LLM-powered features from prototype to production. You will work with domain experts and software engineers to turn ambiguous business problems into measurable, reliable AI capabilities.

## Responsibilities

- Design and implement RAG systems: document ingestion, chunking, embedding, hybrid retrieval, reranking, and grounded answer generation.
- Build agent-style workflows with tool calling, guardrails, and deterministic fallbacks.
- Create evaluation harnesses (golden datasets, LLM-as-judge, regression suites) and monitor quality in production.
- Serve models and inference endpoints efficiently (FastAPI, Docker, GPU/CPU right-sizing, latency/cost budgets).
- Own experiment tracking, model/data versioning, and reproducible training pipelines.
- Collaborate with Security and Legal on PII handling, prompt-injection defenses, and responsible-AI checkpoints.
- Write technical design documents and present trade-offs to engineering and product leadership.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 3+ years of engineering experience, at least 1 year working with LLMs in a production or near-production setting.
- Expert Python; familiarity with PyTorch or TensorFlow for classical deep learning tasks.
- Demonstrated RAG or agent implementations with measurable quality improvements.
- Experience with at least one vector database and with REST API design for ML services.
- Solid understanding of ML evaluation, data leakage risks, and statistical significance.
- Fluent English (working language of the team).

## Nice to Have

- Experience fine-tuning open-source models (Llama, Mistral, Qwen) with LoRA/QLoRA.
- Knowledge of quantization (GGUF, AWQ) and inference engines (vLLM, Ollama, TGI).
- Cloud ML platforms: SageMaker, Vertex AI, or Azure ML.
- Publications, Kaggle track record, or public AI demos.

## Soft Skills

- Intellectual honesty about model failures and limitations.
- Clear communication of technical risk to business stakeholders.
- Collaborative, low-ego approach to code and design reviews.
