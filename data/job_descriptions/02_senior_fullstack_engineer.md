# Senior Full-Stack Engineer

**Department:** Digital Product Engineering  
**Seniority:** Senior  
**Location:** Remote, Romania  
**Work model:** Remote  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English, Romanian

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions builds enterprise-grade digital products for banking, retail, and telecom clients. Our product squads are cross-functional, outcome-driven, and fully remote-first, shipping weekly to production environments used by millions of end users.

## About the Role

As a Senior Full-Stack Engineer you will own features end to end: from UI polish and accessibility, through API design and data modeling, to deployment and monitoring. You will shape engineering standards, review code, and act as a technical counterpart to Product and Design.

## Responsibilities

- Deliver user-facing features across the entire stack (React/TypeScript frontend, Node.js or .NET backend, PostgreSQL).
- Design clean, versioned REST/GraphQL APIs with proper authN/authZ, validation, and error contracts.
- Optimize frontend performance (bundle size, rendering, caching) and backend query performance.
- Write and enforce automated test suites; participate in CI quality gates and code reviews.
- Collaborate in discovery sessions, translate business rules into technical designs, and estimate delivery scope realistically.
- Instrument features with logging, metrics, and error tracking (Sentry/OpenTelemetry).
- Mentor mid-level developers and drive refactoring of legacy modules.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience, with at least 3 years full-stack delivery.
- Expert-level TypeScript and React; solid understanding of the DOM, browser performance, and accessibility (WCAG).
- Production experience with Node.js/NestJS or .NET Web API.
- Strong PostgreSQL skills including migrations and query tuning.
- Experience with Git workflows, code review, and trunk-based or GitFlow branching.
- English proficiency for daily written and spoken communication.

## Nice to Have

- Experience with Next.js, React Query/TanStack, or micro-frontend architectures.
- Familiarity with message brokers (RabbitMQ/Kafka) and event-driven patterns.
- Exposure to AI features: vector databases, embeddings, or LLM API integration.
- Contributions to open source or a public portfolio (GitHub, blog, talks).

## Soft Skills

- Strong product mindset: you challenge requirements when they do not serve users.
- Clear, asynchronous-first communication in a fully remote team.
- Willingness to mentor and to learn from feedback.

## What We Offer

- 100% remote work within Romania, with optional co-working budget.
- Flexible working hours with overlap window 11:00-15:00 EET.
- yearly budget for courses, books, and certifications.
- Private medical insurance, mental-health platform access, and paid sabbatical after 4 years.
- Hardware of your choice and a home-office setup allowance.

## How to Apply

Apply with your CV and, optionally, links to projects or repositories that best represent your work. First round is a 45-minute technical conversation; second round is a practical pairing session.
