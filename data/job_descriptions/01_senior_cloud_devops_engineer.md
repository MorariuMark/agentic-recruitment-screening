# Senior Cloud & DevOps Engineer

**Department:** Infrastructure & Cloud Platforms  
**Seniority:** Senior  
**Location:** Bucharest, Romania / Hybrid  
**Work model:** Hybrid  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English, Romanian

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions is an enterprise IT services partner delivering cloud migration, platform engineering, and AI-enabled operations to clients across the EMEA region. Our internal Platform group owns the golden path: landing zones, developer tooling, and production observability used by 40+ delivery squads.

## About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Cloud & DevOps Engineer to join the Infrastructure & Cloud Platforms team. You will design, automate, and operate our multi-cloud Kubernetes estate, own the CI/CD toolchain, and raise the bar on reliability, cost-efficiency, and security for every workload we run.

## Responsibilities

- Design, deploy, and operate production Kubernetes clusters (AWS EKS / Google GKE) for internal and client-facing workloads.
- Build and maintain infrastructure as code modules in Terraform / OpenTofu, enforcing tagging, policy, and cost guardrails.
- Own CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, ArgoCD) from commit to production, including progressive delivery strategies.
- Implement golden-path developer platforms: self-service environments, scaffolding, secrets management, and artifact registries.
- Define and improve observability: Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards, centralized logging, and distributed tracing.
- Drive incident response, on-call rotations, root-cause analysis, and blameless post-mortems.
- Partner with Security on cloud posture management, IAM hardening, and vulnerability remediation SLAs.
- Mentor mid-level engineers and contribute to architecture decision records (ADRs).

## Minimum Qualifications

- 5+ years of professional experience in DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering roles.
- 4+ years of hands-on Kubernetes administration in production.
- 3+ years of Terraform (or OpenTofu) in multi-environment setups.
- Strong knowledge of at least one major cloud provider (AWS or GCP).
- Experience with Linux systems, networking fundamentals (DNS, TLS, HTTP), and GitOps workflows.
- Working proficiency in English (written and spoken).

## Nice to Have

- Certifications: CKA/CKAD, AWS Solutions Architect Associate/Professional, or GCP equivalent.
- Experience with service mesh (Istio/Linkerd), Kyverno/OPA policies, or Karpenter/Cluster Autoscaler tuning.
- Scripting in Python or Go for automation and internal tooling.
- Exposure to FinOps practices and cloud cost optimization.

## Soft Skills

- Calm and structured communication during production incidents.
- Ownership mindset: you follow through from detection to prevention.
- Ability to write clear documentation and blameless post-mortems.
