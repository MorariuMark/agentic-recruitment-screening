# Security Engineer (Cloud & Application Security)

**Department:** Information Security (CISO Office)  
**Seniority:** Senior  
**Location:** Remote, Romania  
**Work model:** Remote  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English, Romanian

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions serves enterprise clients in finance and telecom where security is a contractual obligation. Our CISO Office runs a product-minded security program: platform guardrails, developer self-service security tooling, and 24/7 detection coverage.

## About the Role

We are hiring a Senior Security Engineer to strengthen cloud and application security across our multi-cloud estate. You will be a hands-on contributor who builds guardrails into the delivery pipeline and partners with engineering teams on remediation.

## Responsibilities

- Harden AWS/Azure/GCP landing zones: IAM design, network policies, encryption, and secret rotation.
- Embed security into CI/CD: SAST/DAST/SCA scanning, IaC policy checks, and image scanning gates.
- Secure Kubernetes workloads: baselines, admission controllers, service mesh mTLS, and runtime monitoring.
- Develop detections and runbooks in the SIEM; participate in on-call incident response.
- Perform threat modeling for new services and security reviews of critical changes.
- Automate compliance evidence collection for ISO 27001 / SOC 2 / NIS2 controls.
- Advise engineers with practical remediation guidance and secure-by-default patterns.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 5+ years in information security, at least 3 in a hands-on engineering capacity.
- Deep knowledge of cloud security controls on AWS, Azure, or GCP.
- Practical experience with the OWASP Top 10, SAST/DAST/SCA tooling, and secrets management (Vault, KMS).
- Production Kubernetes security experience.
- Scripting ability (Python, Bash, or Go) for automation.
- Fluent English.

## Nice to Have

- Certifications: CCSP, AWS Security Specialty, CKS, or OSCP.
- Experience with policy-as-code (OPA/Gatekeeper, Checkov, tfsec).
- Familiarity with NIS2/DORA regulatory expectations in EU IT environments.
- Bug bounty or CTF background.

## Soft Skills

- Influence without authority across delivery squads.
- Clear, calm communication during security incidents.
- Balanced view of risk: pragmatic, not obstructive.

## What We Offer

- Fully remote within Romania with quarterly team gatherings.
- Home-office and security-conference budget.
- Private medical insurance and wellbeing stipend.
- Access to lab environments, bug bounty programs, and training platforms (e.g. Hack The Box, SANS on-demand).
- Direct reporting line to the CISO and visibility at board level.
