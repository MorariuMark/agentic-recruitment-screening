# Senior Data Engineer

**Department:** Data Platform & Analytics  
**Seniority:** Senior  
**Location:** Bucharest, Romania / Hybrid  
**Work model:** Hybrid  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Languages:** English, Romanian

## Company Overview

NovaStack Solutions operates a central Data Platform serving analytics, data science, and AI teams across the organization. We process billions of events per month and power executive dashboards, ML feature stores, and customer-facing data products.

## About the Role

As a Senior Data Engineer you will own critical parts of our lakehouse: ingestion from dozens of sources, transformation layers, and reliable delivery to consumers. You will champion data quality, documentation, and cost awareness while mentoring the team.

## Responsibilities

- Design and maintain scalable batch and streaming ingestion pipelines from APIs, databases, and event buses.
- Build and optimize transformation layers (dbt / Spark) with tests, lineage, and SLA monitoring.
- Model data for analytics and ML consumption: dimensional models, feature tables, and aggregates.
- Orchestrate workflows in Airflow/Dagster with retry, alerting, and idempotency guarantees.
- Manage Snowflake/BigQuery performance: clustering, warehouse sizing, and cost governance.
- Implement data contracts, schema evolution handling, and observability (freshness, volume, null-rate checks).
- Partner with Analytics and AI teams to deliver trustworthy, well-documented datasets.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 5+ years of data engineering or analytics engineering experience.
- Expert-level SQL and strong Python.
- 3+ years orchestrating production pipelines (Airflow or equivalent).
- Experience with a cloud data platform (Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, or Redshift).
- Familiarity with data versioning, CI/CD for data, and Git-based workflows.
- English proficiency for cross-team collaboration.

## Nice to Have

- dbt adoption experience (modular projects, macros, unit tests).
- Kafka/Flink streaming in production.
- Knowledge of GDPR/privacy requirements and PII handling patterns.
- Exposure to ML feature engineering and feature stores.

## Soft Skills

- Pragmatic prioritization between speed and rigor.
- Strong written documentation habits.
- Coaching mindset for mid-level engineers and analysts.

## What We Offer

- Hybrid work in Bucharest (3 days on site) with flexible scheduling.
- Annual budget for trainings, conferences, and certifications.
- Private medical insurance, meal cards, and pension contribution.
- A modern data stack with greenfield migration projects already funded.
- Clear progression path to Staff Data Engineer or Data Platform Lead.
